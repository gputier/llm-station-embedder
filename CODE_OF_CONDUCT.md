# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue with the measurement, not with the person who published it.

## What is expected

- Disagree with the technical claim, and bring what supports your disagreement.
- Accept that a number can be right on one machine and wrong on yours. Say which
  one you measured.
- Assume the other person is doing their best with the hardware and the time
  they have.

## What is not accepted

- Personal attacks, insults, or comments about someone's identity rather than
  their work.
- Harassment of any kind, publicly or in private messages.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Sustained bad faith: reopening a settled question without new evidence,
  or demanding work from a maintainer as if it were owed.

## Enforcement

This is a small personal project. The maintainer will remove comments, close
threads, and block accounts as needed, without a process and without debate.

To report behaviour you consider unacceptable, use GitHub's private
vulnerability reporting on the **Security** tab, or report the account directly
to GitHub. Reports are handled privately.
